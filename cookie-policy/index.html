This policy is published by Global Stack Digital (“Global Stack Digital”, “we”, “us”, “our”) and explains what our website at globalstackdigital.github.io/globalstackdigital stores in your browser. Contact: globalstackdigital@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • This site sets no HTTP cookies of its own.
  • It uses your browser’s local storage (which works like cookies for legal purposes) to remember your cookie choice, your theme preference and a draft of the contact form.
  • Analytics (Mixpanel) loads only after you click Accept. Before that, Mixpanel is not requested and stores nothing in your browser.
  • The site does not use advertising or social-media tracking, and we do not sell data.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google, which is a third-party request (see Section 2).

3. Essential vs. non-essential

  • Essential / functional: your consent choice, theme preference and form draft. They are not tracking and stay on your device.
  • Analytics (non-essential): Mixpanel. Under EU / UK rules (ePrivacy, GDPR, UK PECR) and similar laws, this needs your consent before it is set, so we load it only after you accept.
  • Google Fonts currently loads when a page loads, whatever your choice, because the fonts are served by Google. If you prefer, you can block fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; the site then falls back to default fonts.

4. How the cookie banner works

  1. On your first visit a banner asks whether you accept analytics. You can accept, decline, or open the preferences.
  2. Mixpanel is not requested and writes nothing to your browser until you accept.
  3. Your choice is saved in local storage (gsd-consent) and applied on all pages of this site.
  4. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, analytics start switched off and are loaded only if you turn them on.
  5. You can change your mind at any time with the “Cookie Settings” link in the footer of every page.

5. How to opt out or delete

  • On this site: use the Cookie Settings link here or in the footer to switch analytics off. Switching analytics off stops Mixpanel being loaded on later page loads and removes the Mixpanel entries from your browser.
  • In your browser: clear site data for this website (Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings > View permissions and data stored across sites; Safari: Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data; Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data). This also removes any saved form draft and your saved choices.
  • Block analytics: use browser tracking protection, a content blocker, or enable Global Privacy Control / Do Not Track.
  • Mixpanel: see the Mixpanel privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: you can block fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com with a browser extension.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when we change what we use, and change the “Last updated” date above. Questions: globalstackdigital@gmail.com.
