This policy is published by Global Stack Digital (“Global Stack Digital”, “we”, “us”, “our”) and explains what our website at globalstackdigital.github.io/globalstackdigital stores in your browser. Contact: globalstackdigital@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • This site sets no HTTP cookies of its own.
  • It uses your browser’s local storage (which works like cookies for legal purposes) to remember your theme preference and a draft of the contact form, and for Mixpanel analytics identifiers.
  • Analytics (Mixpanel) runs on every visit. We use Mixpanel to understand how the site is used. There is no cookie banner and no switch on this site to turn analytics off; see Section 5 for ways to limit it.
  • The site does not use advertising or social-media tracking, and we do not sell data.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google, which is a third-party request (see Section 2).

3. Essential vs. non-essential

  • Essential / functional: your theme preference and form draft. They are not tracking and stay on your device.
  • Analytics (non-essential): Mixpanel. It runs on all visits and we do not ask for your consent first. We rely on our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the site (see the Privacy Policy). Some laws require consent for non-essential storage, so please use the options in Section 5 if you do not want it.
  • Google Fonts currently loads on every visit, because the fonts are served by Google. If you prefer, you can block fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com; the site then falls back to default fonts.

4. How analytics works on this site

  1. Mixpanel analytics runs for every visitor on every page load. This site does not show a cookie banner or a preferences panel.
  2. Mixpanel records page views and interactions such as form submission events, together with device and browser information and an approximate location that Mixpanel derives from your IP address.
  3. Mixpanel keeps its identifiers in your browser’s local storage and similar technologies, so it can recognise repeat visits.
  4. Mixpanel processes this data for us as a service provider (processor). We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

5. How to limit tracking or delete data

  • Ask us: email globalstackdigital@gmail.com to ask what we hold about you or to request deletion of data tied to you. We reply within 7 days.
  • In your browser: clear site data for this website (Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Site settings > View permissions and data stored across sites; Safari: Settings > Privacy > Manage Website Data; Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data). This removes the Mixpanel entries, any saved form draft and your theme preference from your browser.
  • Block analytics: use browser tracking protection, a content blocker or ad blocker, or turn on Do Not Track in your browser. We cannot promise that every tool or setting will stop all analytics.
  • Mixpanel: see the Mixpanel privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts: you can block fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com with a browser extension.

6. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when we change what we use, and change the “Last updated” date above. Questions: globalstackdigital@gmail.com.
