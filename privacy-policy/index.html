This policy is published by Global Stack Digital (“Global Stack Digital”, “we”, “us”, “our”). It applies to our website at globalstackdigital.github.io/globalstackdigital. Contact: globalstackdigital@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

Global Stack Digital is a digital growth agency based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India. We offer Digital & Performance Marketing, QA Intelligence & Strategy, and Product Growth Strategy to clients in India, the United States, the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates, Australia and elsewhere. Global Stack Digital operates as a proprietorship. We are not currently registered for GST.

This policy explains what personal data we collect through our website and while delivering services, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have. We try to keep it plain and honest. If something is unclear, write to us.

Who is responsible for your data?

  • For website visitors and enquiries: Global Stack Digital is the “data controller” (GDPR / UK GDPR) and the “Data Fiduciary” (India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, the “DPDP Act”).
  • For client business data we handle while delivering services (for example, your ad accounts, analytics, or test environments): you are generally the controller / Data Fiduciary and Global Stack Digital acts as a processor / Data Processor, working on your documented instructions. See Section 11.

2. What we collect

2.1 Information you give us (contact form)

Our website has a single contact form on the home page. It asks for:

  • Full name (required)
  • Email address (required)
  • Company or brand (optional)
  • Phone / WhatsApp number (optional)
  • Primary service interest (required)
  • Estimated monthly budget (optional)
  • Project goals and requirements (required)

Please do not put sensitive information (such as health data, government ID numbers, passwords or payment card details) in the form. We do not need it to respond.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Analytics (Mixpanel), on all visits: we use Mixpanel to understand how this site is used. It runs for every visitor, and this site does not show a cookie banner or ask you to opt in. Mixpanel records page views and interactions such as the “Lead Submitted” event when you send the contact form (which includes the service you selected). Mixpanel stores an identifier in your browser’s local storage and similar technologies so it can recognise repeat visits. Mixpanel also receives technical data such as device and browser type, operating system, referring page, timestamps and your IP address, from which it derives an approximate location. You can limit this; see Section 7.
  • Form draft (local storage): while you type in the contact form, a draft is saved in your own browser (local storage key gsd-lead-draft) so you do not lose it. It stays on your device and is not sent to us until you submit the form. It is cleared after a successful send.
  • Theme preference (local storage): if you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser (key gsd-theme). It is not sent to us.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts): your browser requests font files from Google’s servers, which means Google receives your IP address and standard request headers. This happens on every visit.
  • Hosting logs: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and user agent).

2.3 Information from emails and calls

If you email us, message us on WhatsApp, or speak with us, we keep the content of those communications and your contact details.

2.4 What we do not do

Our site currently has no user accounts, no newsletter sign-up, and no online payments. We do not collect payment card details through the website. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data through the site.

5. International transfers

We are based in India, and our providers are largely in the United States and other countries. Your data may therefore be processed outside your country of residence.

  • EEA / UK visitors: India does not currently have an EU or UK adequacy decision. Where a transfer mechanism is required, we rely on your own request to contact us and the safeguards our providers make available, such as standard contractual clauses or Data Privacy Framework certification.
  • UAE: transfers are made in line with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (PDPL) and its transfer conditions.
  • Australia: before disclosing personal information overseas we take reasonable steps to ensure the recipient handles it consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles (APP 8).
  • India: the DPDP Act permits transfers outside India except to countries the Government restricts by notification.

You can request details of the safeguards we use by emailing us.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

This site sets no HTTP cookies of its own. We use your browser’s local storage to remember your theme preference and your form draft. Mixpanel analytics runs on every visit and keeps its identifiers in local storage and similar technologies. We also load fonts from Google. This site does not show a cookie banner or ask you to accept analytics. Details are in our Cookie Policy.

How to limit tracking. You can use your browser’s privacy settings, a content blocker or ad blocker, or a Do Not Track setting, and you can clear this site’s data from your browser at any time. You can also email globalstackdigital@gmail.com to ask about, or request deletion of, data tied to you. We reply within 7 days. We cannot promise that every tool or setting will stop all analytics.

Do Not Track / Global Privacy Control. Our own code does not read these signals. We do not sell personal data or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

8. Your rights

Which rights apply depends on where you live. We will honour them for everyone where we reasonably can. To exercise any right, email globalstackdigital@gmail.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within 30 days (or sooner if the law requires); for questions about analytics data or deletion requests we reply within 7 days.

8.1 India (DPDP Act, 2023; IT Act, 2000 and SPDI Rules, 2011)

As a Data Principal you have the right to:

  • obtain a summary of the personal data we process, the processing activities, and the identities of those we share it with;
  • correct, complete, update or erase your personal data;
  • withdraw consent at any time (withdrawal is as easy as giving consent and does not affect earlier lawful processing; we may be unable to continue a service that depends on the data);
  • grievance redressal, and to nominate another person to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Please use our grievance process first (Section 14). If unresolved, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted and operational for such complaints.

8.2 EEA and UK (GDPR / UK GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection (including to processing based on legitimate interests), and to withdraw consent. We do not carry out solely automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority, for example the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk) or the data protection authority in your EU member state. We would appreciate the chance to resolve your concern first.

8.3 California, USA (CCPA/CPRA, CalOPPA)

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, and have not done so in the preceding 12 months. If the CCPA applies to us, California residents may request to know, access, correct and delete their personal information, and will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. As CalOPPA requires, this policy describes the categories of data we collect and how we treat Do Not Track signals (Section 7). Authorised agents may submit requests on your behalf with proof of authority. Residents of other US states with privacy laws may use the same process.

8.4 United Arab Emirates (PDPL)

Under Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability, and may object to processing or withdraw consent. You may complain to the UAE Data Office.

8.5 Australia (Privacy Act 1988 and APPs)

You may request access to and correction of your personal information (APPs 12 and 13). We aim to handle personal information consistently with the Australian Privacy Principles. If you are unhappy with our response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal data, including collecting only what we need, limiting access to the people who need it, using reputable providers, and serving our site over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. See Security & Data Handling. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and any authority as the law requires (in India, CERT-In and the Data Protection Board; elsewhere, the relevant regulator).

10. Children

Our website and services are for businesses and are not directed at children. Under the DPDP Act a “child” is anyone under 18; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children and do not track or target them. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Client data handled during engagements

When we work with you on marketing, QA or growth projects we may access:

  • advertising, analytics and CMS accounts (for example Google Ads, Analytics, Search Console, social platforms);
  • customer lists, leads and campaign data;
  • staging or production environments, test accounts and test data.

In these cases we act on your instructions as your processor. We will: use the data only to deliver the agreed services; limit access to those who need it; keep it confidential; not use it for our own purposes; delete or return it at the end of the engagement; and help you respond to data-subject requests. We ask that you give us only the access and data we need, and use test or masked data wherever possible. A Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available on request and should be signed before we handle regulated personal data. You are responsible for having a lawful basis and notices in place for data you give us.

12. Third-party links

Our site may link to other websites, including our social profiles. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, give notice on the site or by email. Where the law requires fresh consent, we will ask for it.
